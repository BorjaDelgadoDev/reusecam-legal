Privacy policy

App: ReuseCam (com.borjadelgado.reusecam) · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who is responsible

ReuseCam is published by Borja Delgado. You can contact me about this policy or your data at borjadelgadodev@gmail.com.

What the app does

ReuseCam turns an old Android phone into a home camera. The phone captures video with its camera and shows it in the web browser of another device that is on the same Wi-Fi network and opens the app's private link.

Short version

  • I do not collect your data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of mine.
  • The video never leaves your home network. It is not recorded, not stored and not sent to me or to any third party.

Data the app handles

  • Camera video. The app uses the camera only while you start a stream. The video goes straight from your phone to the browsers on the same Wi-Fi network that open your private link. It is shown live; the app does not save it.
  • The private link code. The app creates a random code that is part of the link used to watch. It is stored in a file inside the app on your phone. It is excluded from Android backups and is not copied to a new phone.
  • A cookie in the browser that watches. When a device opens your link, the app asks its browser to keep an access cookie, so it does not need the link again. The cookie lasts up to 400 days and is renewed each time the page is used. It only lets that browser open the stream of your phone.
  • Network information. The app reads whether the phone is on Wi-Fi and its local address, so it can show the link. This information stays on the phone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to capture the video you stream.
  • Notifications: to show that a stream is running. Optional.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state, and internet access: to work out the address and to serve the video to other devices on your network.
  • Foreground service (camera) and wake lock: to keep the stream running while the screen is off.

Who can see the stream

Anyone who has your private link and is on the same Wi-Fi network can watch the video. Treat the link like a password. In the app, "Regenerate link" creates a new code, disconnects everyone who was watching and makes the old link and cookies stop working.

Sharing with third parties

I do not share data with anyone. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

  • The video is not stored, so there is nothing to keep or delete.
  • The link code stays on your phone until you regenerate it (the old one is discarded) or uninstall the app, which deletes it.
  • The cookie lives in the browser of the device that watches. You can delete it from that browser's settings, or make it useless by regenerating the link.
  • I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to delete on my side. If you write to me by email, I only use your message to answer you.

Children

ReuseCam is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, I will update this page and the date above.
